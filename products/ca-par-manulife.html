Manulife Par

The Manufacturers Life Insurance Company

Manulife Canada's participating whole life plan. Premiums, lifetime cover and cash values are guaranteed, non-guaranteed annual dividends can grow the cover and cash value, and you can make extra deposits without underwriting.

01Product overview

In OSFI's 2025 life insurers' annual returns (LF3), Manulife (The Manufacturers Life Insurance Company) ranked second for the face amount of new participating individual insurance, at C$16.5bn.[1] The whole life list on the company's official website has two plans, 'Manulife Par with Vitality Plus' and 'Manulife Par'; the first adds a health rewards programme with a monthly fee (Vitality Plus), so we show the base plan.[2]

The company describes Manulife Par as a participating whole life plan with guaranteed premiums, cover that lasts for life, cash value that grows over time and guaranteed rates for buying extra insurance.[3]

02How it works

STEP 1
Paying premiums
You pay for the period you choose: 10 or 20 years, or to age 90 or 100. Premiums are guaranteed.
STEP 2
The participating account
Premiums are pooled in the company's participating account and invested, and a non-guaranteed dividend is set each year according to its results.
STEP 3
Using dividends
Dividends buy paid-up additions to grow cover and cash value, or are paid in cash.
STEP 4
Extra deposits and access
Within a pre-approved lifetime limit you can make extra deposits (the deposit option) without underwriting to grow cover and cash value, and you can access the cash value when you need it.

03Key features

What is guaranteed

Premiums, lifetime cover, cash values, and the rates for buying paid-up and deposit option insurance and their cash value rates.

Deposit option

Extra payments on top of regular premiums grow cover and cash values. There is no underwriting within the pre-approved lifetime limit, including for policies converted from term.

Coverage options

Single life or joint last-to-die (premiums to the last death).

No policy fees

There are no extra policy fees.

Optional riders

You can add 10 and 20-year renewable term insurance riders (TIR), total disability waivers for the insured and payor (TDW), a guaranteed insurability option (GIO) and a children's protection rider (CPR).

Manulife Vitality

Single-life policies on insured people aged 18 or over get the Vitality Go health rewards programme at no extra cost, and can upgrade to Vitality Plus without underwriting before the 3rd policy anniversary.

04Risks and points to note

  • Dividends are not promisesDividends are non-guaranteed, set each year and depend on the results of the company's participating account. The dividend scale interest rate also changes each year.
  • Losses on early surrenderThe plan is designed to be held for the long term, so if you surrender early you may get back less than you paid.
  • High minimum coverCover starts at C$100,000 (C$500,000 for pay to 100), so you cannot take it out for a small amount.
  • Exchange ratesCover and cash values are in Canadian dollars, so the value in won depends on exchange rates.

05Checkpoints for Korean residents

  • Limits on offering and arranging it in KoreaBeing offered this product in Korea, or buying it through an intermediary, is restricted in principle.Korea's Insurance Business Act, Article 3
  • For Canadian residentsIndividual life insurance in Canada is generally for Canadian residents. It is best seen as information for families who have emigrated or live there long term.
  • InheritanceCheck with a tax adviser how an overseas insurance payout forms part of an estate in Korea and how it is reported.
  • Overseas financial account reportingCheck whether accounts and policies held with overseas financial institutions must be reported.National Tax Service · Overseas financial account reporting
  • Tax on insurance gainsIf what you receive on surrender or maturity exceeds the premiums you paid, the gain is taxed in Korea as interest income, and the special rule that excludes Korean savings policies held for 10 years or more from tax does not apply to overseas policies taken out under the proviso to Article 3 of the Insurance Business Act.Korea's Income Tax Act, Article 16 · Enforcement Decree, Article 25
