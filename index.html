BEFORE YOU START

Three things Korean residents should check first

1

Overseas insurance cannot be offered or arranged to you in Korea

Concluding or arranging an insurance contract with anyone other than a licensed insurer (including foreign insurers without a Korean licence) is restricted in principle, and the exceptions set out in the Enforcement Decree are narrow.Korea's Insurance Business Act, Article 3 · Korea Law Information Center

2

Overseas funds not registered in Korea cannot be sold in Korea

To sell foreign collective investment securities in Korea, they must be registered with the Financial Services Commission. Each product page says so separately.Korea's Financial Investment Services and Capital Markets Act, Article 279

3

Balances held abroad may have to be reported

If the combined balance of accounts you hold with overseas financial institutions exceeds KRW 500 million on the last day of any month, you must report it in June of the following year. This depends on your circumstances, so please check with a tax adviser.National Tax Service · Overseas financial account reporting
