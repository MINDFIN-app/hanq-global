ABOUT HANQ

So you can start with knowledge of overseas finance

Using statistics from regulators and industry associations in five countries, hanQ Global Finance picks the insurance and investment products that actually sell the most and sets out their structure and risks, along with the tax and reporting points Korean residents need to watch. We go through any questions with you in a consultation.

WHAT WE DO

What we do

01

Sales-ranking research by country

We use official statistics to pick the top three insurance and investment sellers in Hong Kong, Singapore, the UK, Canada and Japan, and write up each product. For insurance we also show representative products chosen from companies' official websites.

02

One-to-one consultations

We talk through the countries and products you are interested in, going over our research and the original sources together.

03

Guidance on tax and reporting checks

We point out items to prepare for, such as overseas financial account reporting and the taxation of overseas products. For tax judgements, we ask you to check with a professional.

04

Check-ins after you buy

When the rankings or market data change, we let you know about changes that affect the products you hold.

HOW IT WORKS

How a consultation works

  1. EnquiryUse the enquiry form to tell us the countries and products you are interested in and what you would like to know.
  2. ContactWe contact you on the details you left within two working days.
  3. ConsultationWe explain the structure and risks, going over our research and the original sources together.
  4. MaterialsWe send you the sources and checkpoints covered in the consultation.
  5. You decideWhether and how to buy is for you to judge and decide.
WHAT WE DON'T DO

What we don't do

  • 1

    We do not guarantee your capital or any return

    Overseas financial products carry currency, credit and liquidity risk, and you can lose money.

  • 2

    We do not offer or arrange products that cannot be sold in Korea

    Selling overseas insurance in Korea and selling overseas funds not registered in Korea are restricted by law. We provide product information and checkpoints.

  • 3

    We do not advise on ways to avoid tax

    We only cover the information you need to meet your tax and reporting obligations on overseas assets.
