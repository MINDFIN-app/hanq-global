PRIVACY

Privacy policy

hanQ Global Finance collects only the minimum personal data needed to answer enquiries. This policy is a draft awaiting compliance review, and items not yet confirmed are marked. This English version is a translation; the Korean version prevails.

01What we collect and how

RequiredName, mobile number, your enquiry
OptionalEmail, countries of interest, product of interest, preferred call time
MethodEntered by you in the enquiry form on this website

We do not collect information such as how much you plan to invest, the size of your assets or your resident registration number.

02Purpose

Receiving and answering enquiries and contacting you. We do not use the data for any other purpose, and if the purpose changes we will ask for your consent again.

03Retention and deletion

Retention period: six months from the date we receive your enquiry. When the period ends, or if you ask us to delete your data, we delete it without delay. Electronic files are deleted so that they cannot be recovered.

04Disclosure to third parties

We do not give your personal data to third parties, except when required by law.

05Processing by others and transfers abroad

What you send through the enquiry form passes through Google Apps Script, provided by Google LLC, and is delivered to and stored in hanQ Global Finance's Gmail account. Your personal data may therefore be transferred abroad (to Google's data-processing facilities). The data transferred is the same as listed in section 01; it is sent over the network when you submit the form and is kept for the retention period in section 03. If you do not want your data transferred abroad, you can ask to be contacted another way, such as by phone.

06Your rights

You can ask at any time to see, correct or delete your personal data, or to stop us processing it. Send your request to the officer named below and we will deal with it without delay.

08Effective date

This policy takes effect on To be confirmed · effective date.
